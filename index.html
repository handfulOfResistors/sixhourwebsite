Common questions

Is the site really done in six hours?

Yes. The six hours start when we confirm your order and have your photos. If we miss the deadline, the site is free.

How long does the Black Friday price last?

Through November 30. Any order placed by then gets the $199 price, even if we build your site a few days later. After that, a site costs $499.

When do I pay?

After the site is finished and you’ve looked it over. We don’t take a deposit. When you approve it, we send a secure payment link and you pay by card.

What if I don’t like the site?

Then you don’t pay, and you have no obligation to us. You can also ask for changes first.

Are the domain and hosting included?

The $199 covers building the site and connecting it to your domain. Hosting and updates are $19 a month. The domain name is paid separately to the registrar.

What do you need from me?

The order form, your logo if you have one, and photos of your work. If you don’t have a logo, we’ll set your business name in clean lettering.

Can I change things later?

Yes. With the $19 monthly plan, send us new prices, photos, or text and we’ll update the site for you.

What kinds of businesses do you build for?

Local service businesses: landscaping, cleaning, auto detailing, handyman work, painting, roofing, plumbing, and similar trades.
